# How the evening reflection works

**What:** a two-minute daily check-in at 5 pm, then agents work while I sleep.

**Flow:**
1. **4:56 pm:** a scheduled agent reads this morning's brief, what I actually did (sent email, calendar, Linear, captures) and the last week's reflections. It posts one message to `#personal`: Top 3 status, one friction with evidence and a fix, and a numbered overnight queue.
2. **Any time before about 8:45 pm:** I reply in the thread, by voice or text: a 1–5 rating, a line on the day, and which queue items to run. Slack capture saves the reply to the Brain and answers ✓.
3. **8:45 pm, silently:** a second agent reads my reply, saves a structured reflection to the Brain, and runs only the approved items, at research and draft level (Gmail drafts, never sent). It writes an `Overnight results` note.
4. **3:47 am:** the morning brief shows the overnight results.

**Why two routines:** the first needs me; the second needs my answer. Splitting them keeps quiet hours quiet and means nothing runs without my OK.

**Teach it in one line:** the OS asks three small questions at the end of the day, then works on tomorrow while I sleep, and every answer becomes data about how I work.
